Ref 1 @huntergrazianoo 29s The return angle
What works
The "definitely returning" angle, which I keep seeing: "why am I returning this item?" It is never actually a bad thing. It is negative marketing that is positive all the way through. Not because of this benefit, not because of that one. It is because I got it when they were not on sale, and now they are. The scream on the hook calls attention and sets the fast pace.
How we adapt it
  1. Scream.
  2. "I am definitely returning this compression tank"
  3. Benefit 1 · e.g. ("But it is not because it hides the gut and the man boobs.")
  4. Benefit 2 · e.g. ("Not because it is super breathable and expandable.")
  5. Benefit 3 · e.g. ("And not because it blocks sweat in the heat.")
  6. It is because they are cheaper now (feel free to put your own words into this)
  7. Call to action
